# Support Operations Intelligence Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Support Operations Intelligence Platform** is an end-to-end data analytics project developed to analyze IT support ticket data and provide actionable business insights into customer support operations. Inspired by enterprise service management platforms such as **Freshworks, Zendesk, and ServiceNow**, this project demonstrates how data analytics can improve operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and decision-making.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow—from data preprocessing and SQL-based business analysis to interactive Power BI dashboards.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations receive thousands of customer support tickets daily through multiple channels such as Email, Chat, Phone, and Web Forms.

Without a centralized analytics solution, support managers face challenges in:

* Monitoring support performance
* Identifying operational bottlenecks
* Tracking customer satisfaction (CSAT)
* Reducing ticket resolution time
* Optimizing support channels
* Prioritizing product improvements

This project provides an interactive Business Intelligence solution to address these challenges.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze customer support ticket trends.
* Measure operational efficiency using business KPIs.
* Evaluate customer satisfaction across multiple dimensions.
* Identify high-impact issue categories and product areas.
* Compare support channel performance.
* Build executive dashboards for business decision-making.
